# 37 — Meaning Demo (10 seconds)

A short scripted scene that shows how change in one property reads as meaning.
Three nodes appear ahead of you: A, B (a neighbour) and C.

| Time | Axiom | What changes | What it reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5–3.5 s | Power ⚡ | A grows and glows | strength |
| 3.5–5.5 s | Time ⧖ | B spins faster | urgency |
| 5.5–7.5 s | Expression ❖ | C shifts cool → warm | mood |
| 7.5–9.5 s | Space ▦ | A and B move apart | distance |

Run it
- Ribbon: Sandbox > Demos > Meaning demo
- Command: `Omni Demo play name(meaning)`
- No captions (for testing with other people): `Omni Demo play name(meaning) captions(false)`
- Stop early: Esc, or `Omni Demo stop`
- List demos: `Omni Demo list`

The demo nodes delete themselves at the end. Your own nodes are untouched.
