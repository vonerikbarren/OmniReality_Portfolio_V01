# Axioms, Perspectives and Dimensions (V217)

Open **⟐OmniDimensions** from the ribbon (◇), OmniStarLight ("dimension", "axiom"), or the nav item. The same pickers sit in the hand tool menus.

## Axioms (OmniHand) and perspectives (ConsciousHand)
The OmniHand holds the higher-order setting, the **axiom**: Identity, Information, Space, Time, Power, Expression (in that order). The ConsciousHand then offers **perspectives of that axiom**; change the axiom and the perspective list changes with it. Each axiom remembers the perspective you last used on it.

- OmniHand tools menu (the chip on its pad) has the **Axiom** picker.
- ConsciousHand tools menu has the **Perspective** picker for the current axiom (and the multiplier box when the perspective has one).

**Time really works.** Perspectives: *Moment* (motion holds still), *Real time*, *Hyperlapse ×N*, *Slow-mo ×N*, *Bullet time ×N*, *Looped event* (a stretch of N seconds that repeats). It scales OmniVibration, node behaviours, the tunnel particle streams and the Dimensional Node cubes. The camera, UI and pads stay in real time so you can always move. Leave Time and motion returns to normal. The other axioms' perspectives are labels for now; Dimensional Nodes already react to all of them. The perspective lists are a first draft in `data/OmniAxiomData.js`.

## Dimensional nodes
A glass cube, a pocket tuned to one channel (an axiom and a perspective, or "any" perspective). While your view doesn't match it stays veiled (a faint "?"). When it matches, it opens: the inside is x-rayed (a spinning frame, a core and a satellite) and its head signature appears. Inspector: pick its axiom/perspective, signature, and whether it shows the x-ray or only the signature; "Tune to what I am viewing" and "Tune my view to this node". A blank axiom means "inherit the channel of my Space".

The first time you run V217 in a space with none, **twelve demo nodes** are scattered around the origin (two or three per axiom, four on Time: any, Moment, Hyperlapse, Slow-mo). Try each axiom and each Time perspective and watch them open and spin at the new speed. **Scatter demo** / **Remove demo** in the panel, **+ At the camera** makes your own.

## Spaces and planes
A **space** is a named pocket holding **planes** (layers). Select a node, then "+ node" on a plane. A plane can be **hidden** (its nodes vanish) or **locked** (behaviours stop moving them). A space can carry a **default channel** that its blank-axiom Dimensional Nodes inherit. Deleting a plane moves its nodes to another; deleting a space or taking a node out restores it.
