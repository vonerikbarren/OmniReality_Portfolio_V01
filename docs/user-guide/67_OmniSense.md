# OmniSense (basic layout)

OmniSense shows your voxel rooms as tiles on one flat 3D plane. Every room carries an OmniVon, a small equation that says what the room is.

**Open it:** ribbon button (the square with lines) or `Omni Sense open`. Add rooms first in the OmniVoxel panel.

**Views:** Top down looks straight at the plane. Isometric looks from a corner. Wide orbit stands back. After the camera arrives the mouse orbits as usual, so you can look around any view.

**Rooms:** click a tile, or a room name in the panel. The equation shows on the OmniTeleprompt. The room always gives the Room term and its name. You can add terms, for example `⧖:"Real time"`. The place stays the room's. Reset goes back to what the room gives.

**Commands:** `Omni Sense open view(top)`, `Omni Sense view iso`, `Omni Sense von room(0,0,0)`, `Omni Sense von room(0,0,0) text(...)`, `Omni Sense close`.
