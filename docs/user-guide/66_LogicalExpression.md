# LogicalExpression

Your movement signature: the hyperlapses and teleport steps that play after the fall, and again as a reward when you finish something. It always ends with you placed in the scene, facing the way you were.

Open the panel with the ribbon button. Tempo, intensity, how many teleport steps against hyperlapses, hops, reach, vibration, colour and sound shape it; **Derive from my work** draws a signature from what you have built. **Preview** plays it.

- **Skip**: press Space, then click Skip twice.
- **Calm**: the same signature without flashing or shaking. It is used automatically when your system asks for reduced motion.
- **Any tool can play it**: `Omni LogicalExpression play kind(success) title(Done)` (works in OmniIDE), or the event `omni:logical-expression-play`.

The sound cues go out as `omni:logical-expression-cue` for the sound system to answer.
