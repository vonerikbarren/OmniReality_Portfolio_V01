# The starting scene is clean (V239)

The loose decoration shapes are no longer created: the cross of 7 shapes (four cardinal arms, centre, up, down), the 5 portal spheres with their orbit rings, and the 2 moving spheres with trails. The OmniBrowser layered gridSphere, the Platform rings, the targeting reticle and everything you build are untouched.

They are kept in the code. To bring one back: `Omni Decor show name(landingRoom)` (or `portalSpheres`, `trackingNodes`), then reload. `Omni Decor hide name(...)` turns it off again; `Omni Decor list` shows the state.

The NavMap fast-travel list was built from the cross of shapes, so it has no entries while that is off.
