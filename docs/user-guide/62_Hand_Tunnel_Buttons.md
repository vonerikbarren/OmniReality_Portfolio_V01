# LH and RH tunnel buttons (V282)

The OmniHand and ConsciousHand pads have a ◎ button that shows or hides their tunnel. The LogicalHand and CreativeHand pads also have tunnels (Λ and Ψ, view only: their pads move the camera), but their Activate button fires ammo, so they had no tunnel button. V282 adds one.

- **On the pad:** a round ◎ TUNL button on the LH and RH pads, one step before Release (up and toward the middle of the screen). Lit while the tunnel is shown. On a phone there is no room, so use the ribbon.
- **Ribbon:** Hands tab, "Fire & tunnels": **LH tunnel** (Λ) and **RH tunnel** (Ψ), lit while shown.
- **Terminal:** `Omni Tunnel toggle hand(lh)` (also rh, omnihand, conscious).
- A tunnel still shows by itself while its pad is open when "Tunnels follow pads" is on; the new button overrides that (it hides it until the pad is next toggled, or pins it on).
