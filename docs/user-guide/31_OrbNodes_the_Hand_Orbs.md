# OrbNodes: the orb of every hand (V234)

Each hand has an **orb** that sits **behind its ⬢ tools menu**. Open it with the hand's **⦿ Orb-it** cell (or `Omni Orb open hand(rh)`). The old ◉ ORB button is gone.

## What an orb shows
- **Words = potentials**: what you could do. About 30 synonyms around the hand's idea, plus the same idea in the other top-20 languages (a language test). A dozen are in view and one swaps at random every second or so.
- **Tools streaming into the core = what is available right now.**

| Hand | Idea (potentials) | Tools (available) |
|---|---|---|
| LH | possibility (greyscale) | the magazine |
| RH | making: Ideas, Colors, Materials, Textures, Vectors, Points ... | the magazine |
| ConsciousHand | perspective: Perspectives, Previews, Insights, Philosophies, Eyesights ... | its pad options |
| OmniHand | the OmniProducts (OmniStar product list) | the products you can open now; the ones you cannot yet stay in the orbit, dim, with the reason |

## Using it
- **Click a word** to choose it (it stays in view). **Click a tool**: LH/RH load it as ammo; OmniHand opens that product.
- **Hold the core, release**: LH/RH fire the loaded ammo. ConsciousHand and OmniHand **summon** an orb into the scene; a fuller charge makes a bigger one.
- **✦ Summon** puts an orb **in the scene**, 8 units in front of you: a core node you can select, move or delete, with the chosen words looping around it. (What a combination of words does in the scene is not built yet. Scene orbs are not saved with the scene yet.)
- Drag the rim to move an orb.

## Look: ⟐OmniHands → the hand → ⦿ Orb-it
Words (and Reset to the full list), words shown at once, orbit speed, panel colour, opacity, and **one picture or video per hand** (pictures up to 8 MB, videos up to 40 MB, kept in this browser).

## Omni commands
`Omni Orb open|close [hand(lh|rh|conscious|omnihand)]` · `Omni Orb words hand(rh) value("A, B")` (value(reset) restores) · `Omni Orb summon hand(rh) words("Ideas, Colors") [pos(x, y, z)] [scale(1..3)]` · `Omni Orb list` · `Omni Orb clear confirm` · `Omni Orb fire hand(lh|rh) [charge(0..1)]`
