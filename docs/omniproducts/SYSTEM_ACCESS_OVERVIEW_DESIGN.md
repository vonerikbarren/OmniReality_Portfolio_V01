# System-Level Access & Permission Overview (name not yet decided)

Raised as explicitly *not* what OmniComplexity does — worth
confirming that distinction directly, since both could sound similar
from a one-line description. Checked against both existing
candidates before writing this: it isn't `OMNICOMPLEXITY_DESIGN.md`
(that's multi-axis *structural* navigation — hierarchical/
categorical/spectral relationships between things), and it isn't
`core-systems/OMNICONFIG_DESIGN.md` either (that's named similarly but
is actually about text styling, unrelated). This is a third, distinct
thing, with no existing home — hence a new doc rather than folding it
into either.

## The core idea

A general-structure overview of the logic within one reality, in its
ObjectiveRealityForm — described directly as being like a table of
contents or a tree, but organized by **availability and access**, not
by complexity or category. For a given reality, this would show:

- What systems are available within it.
- The data around each one.
- The permission level required to navigate to it.
- The access level of the OmniProduct itself.

Long-term-goal framing, not near-term — raised as "later down the
road," not something queued for immediate build.

## Why this is genuinely different from OmniComplexity

OmniComplexity's own doc is explicit that it's about structural
*shape* — how things relate to each other (parent/child, category,
spectrum position). This new concept isn't about shape at all — it's
about **permission and availability**: not "how is this organized"
but "what am I allowed to see or reach, and at what tier." A
structurally simple reality could still have complex permission
rules, and a structurally complex one could be fully open — the two
axes are independent of each other.
