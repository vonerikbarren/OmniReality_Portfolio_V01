# OmniBrand

Nothing built. Raised directly as a root-object concept — described
by comparison to "the reality of Apple" or "Samsung and its reality":
a brand isn't just a logo or a style guide here, it's treated as its
own root-level reality-equivalent, the same conceptual tier as any
other OmniReality-scoped thing.

## The core idea

A standard Tree Building System, serving two audiences at once by
design, not as two separate products:

- **A Map, for simpler users** — a legible, low-friction way to see
  "what is this brand/OS made of," without needing to understand the
  underlying structure.
- **A Navigation reference, for complex users** — the same tree,
  usable as a real way to move through and reach specific things
  precisely.

Explicitly framed as important that the OS serve both kinds of user
at once — this isn't a simple-mode/advanced-mode split into two
different tools, it's one structure meant to read correctly both
ways.

## Where this connects to what already exists

- **OmniComplexity** (`OMNICOMPLEXITY_DESIGN.md`) already does multi-
  axis structural navigation — hierarchical/categorical/spectral.
  Whether OmniBrand's tree *is* an application of OmniComplexity's
  own hierarchical axis, specifically scoped to brand/root-identity,
  or a genuinely separate structure, isn't decided yet.
- **The System-Level Access & Permission Overview**
  (`SYSTEM_ACCESS_OVERVIEW_DESIGN.md`) is also table-of-contents-
  shaped, but organized by permission/access rather than brand
  identity — likely a different tree over similar underlying data,
  not the same tree twice, but worth checking once both are further
  along.

## Status

Purely conceptual — name, root-level framing, and the dual-audience
requirement are the only things settled. No tree structure, no data
model, no UI.
