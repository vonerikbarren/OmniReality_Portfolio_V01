# The 32 — Glyph Candidates, Operators First

A real, reasoned proposal following `THE_32_REVISED_PROPOSAL.md` — names
and interfaces settled there, glyphs designed here. Every candidate is
scored against one real standard: **would a human with zero shared
culture or language — a total knowledge reset, not just a different
country — plausibly decode this from its form alone?** This is the
same bar nuclear semiotics (the 1981 Human Interference Task Force,
tasked with warning people 10,000 years from now) and the Voyager
Golden Record held themselves to: lean on physics, embodied experience,
and geometry — never on having learned a specific writing system.

**Tier 1** = genuine iconicity — the shape's meaning comes from its
own form (a point, a line, an enclosure, a direction).
**Tier 2** = borrowed convention — works because of shared modern/
technical culture (Greek letters as math notation, Cyrillic letters),
not because the shape itself carries the meaning. Still useful and
still proposed in places, but labeled honestly rather than oversold.

## Part 1 — Operators (the grammar, not the vocabulary)

The real finding underneath ◬ш (Progression's confirmed legacy glyph,
root + modifier) is that this system doesn't need one glyph per axiom
— it needs a small set of **roots** and a small set of **operators**
that combine with any root predictably, the way a prime mark or an
exponent modifies a base symbol in math, or a radical plus a phonetic
component combines in Chinese writing.

| Operator | Glyph | Meaning | Tier | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existence / Activation | **⟐** | "this is instantiated / brought into being" | 2, pragmatically confirmed | Already your established operator — Omni(x) already reads as "the existence of x." Not pure iconicity, but it's load-bearing and shouldn't be redesigned just for purity. |
| Escalation | **′** (tier 1), **″** (tier 2) | "more of this, one degree up" | 1 *and* 2, dual-justified | Repeated marks for "more" is one of the oldest, most independently-invented conventions in counting (tally strokes appear in unrelated cultures worldwide) — genuinely Tier 1. It also happens to match existing math notation (f′, f″) which is a bonus, not the reason it works. |
| Negation / Absence | **⊘** | "the absence of this" | 2, leaning 1 | A slash through a shape (cancel, stop, prohibit) traces back to a basic motor gesture — physically crossing something out — so it's plausibly closer to Tier 1 than most borrowed symbols, even though its specific circle-slash form is a 20th-century international-signage convention. |
| Inversion / Polarity | **mirror the glyph**, or **~** when the glyph has no natural mirror | "the opposite expression of this root" | 1 when mirrored, 2 when using ~ | Mirroring is the strongest option where possible — an arrow flips (→ becomes ←) with zero learned convention required. The ~ fallback is Tier 2, needed only for symmetric shapes that can't meaningfully flip. |

This is the part that actually resolves old open problems, not just
adds new symbols:

- **Attract / Radiate** no longer need to be two unrelated glyphs
  fighting over one product (the standing tension in both earlier
  alphabet docs). One root — a field/pull-push shape — plus the
  Inversion operator gives you both for free.
- **Progression / Regression** — same move. One root (a stepping/
  ascending shape), Inversion operator flips it to descending.
- **SpaceInternal / SpaceExternal** — one root (a boundary), Inversion
  (or simply: point-inside vs. point-outside the same boundary) gives
  both without needing two unrelated shapes.

## Part 2 — Root glyph candidates, scored

| Axiom | Candidate | Tier | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existence | ⟐ *(doubles as operator)* | 2 | See above. |
| Begin | **•** (a single open point) | 1 | The most primitive mark in every independently-invented counting system — a single point before anything extends from it. |
| End | **●** (a single filled point) | 1 | Deliberately paired with Begin — open vs. filled is a near-zero-learning-curve distinction (empty/full, potential/realized). Replaces Ω's borrowed-convention status with a Tier 1 pair. |
| Structure | **⌗** (a simple lattice/cross-hatch) | 1 | A grid is the most basic constructive/load-bearing visual — scaffolding, lattice, and frame independently appear everywhere humans build things. |
| Express | **radiating short lines from a point** | 1 | "Something emerging outward from within" — close cousin of how emission/radiation is drawn in every independently-developed physics tradition. |
| Illuminate | **a point with longer rays** (sun-like, simplified) | 1 | Sun-as-radiating-point symbols arise independently in virtually every ancient culture (Egyptian, Aztec, Chinese) without contact — about as strong a Tier 1 claim as exists. |
| Attract / Radiate | **a field shape (converging lines)** + Inversion operator for the outward form | 1 | Convergence = pull, divergence = push — both map directly to observable physical phenomena (gravity, light) independently discoverable. |
| PrimaryForce | **a bold directional arrow** | 1 | Arrows-as-force/direction are arguably the single strongest cross-cultural iconic candidate of this whole list — tied to pointing and throwing, near-universal embodied gestures. |
| Freedom | **an open, unclosed curve** | 1 | Open vs. closed shapes mapping to free vs. bound is intuitive and pairs cleanly with Containment's closed form. |
| Progression / Regression | **an ascending step shape** + Inversion for descending | 1 | Physical stepping/climbing is one of the most universal embodied human experiences there is. |
| Desire | **an open curve reaching toward a point** | 2, honestly weak | Internal mental states are the hardest category to iconify at all — flagged here rather than oversold. |
| Problem | **a tangled/crossed knot shape** | 1 | Physical knots are a near-universal human object and "stuck-ness" metaphor. |
| Solution | **the same shape, untangled into a straight line** | 1 | Direct visual complement to Problem — tangled vs. straight. |
| Energy | **a jagged/zigzag line** | 1 | Lightning is a directly observed natural phenomenon independent of culture — the strongest possible grounding for a symbol. |
| Motion | **an arrow with trailing streak lines** | 1 | Motion blur is something every sighted human perceives directly, not something taught. |
| Perspective | **a simple eye shape (two meeting curves)** | 1 | Eyes are a universal biological feature; eye-shapes for "seeing/viewpoint" appear independently across cultures. |
| Time | **a circle with one radius line** (clock-like) | 1 | Circular time-tracking (sundials, cyclical calendars) was independently invented by essentially every ancient civilization that tracked time at all. |
| SpaceInternal / SpaceExternal | **a boundary shape** with a point inside vs. outside | 1 | Enclosure + interior/exterior point is about as basic as spatial relationships get. |
| Variable | **a single tally mark** | 1 | A single countable unit — same logic as Begin, but repeatable/multipliable. |
| Data | **a cluster of tally marks** | 1 | Many countable units together — direct extension of Variable. |
| Complexity | **a dense web of crossing lines** | 1 | More intersections read as more complexity with no cultural learning required. |
| Vector | **a plain arrow** | 1 | Direction + magnitude as an arrow is literally how physics notation converged globally, because it's the most direct way to draw the concept. |
| Value | **a balance/scale shape** (two pans on a beam) | 1 | Independently invented for trade and weighing across Egyptian, Chinese, Roman, and other unconnected civilizations — one of the strongest Tier 1 candidates here, and thematically exact for OmniValue. |
| Flow | **a wavy, sinuous line** | 1 | Water-current symbols appear independently almost everywhere humans settled near water. |
| Conflict | **two crossing lines (an X) or opposing arrows meeting** | 1 | Crossed paths/blades as a conflict visual is close to a basic motor-level gesture. |
| Relationship | **↔** (a line connecting two points) | 1 | Already in the legacy list, and about as strong a Tier 1 candidate as exists — a line between two things is a connection, full stop. |
| Causation | **an arrow from one distinct shape to another** | 1 | Directly extends the pointing/throwing gesture argument — "this leads to that," spatially drawn exactly as it's conceptually understood. |
| Experience | **layered/overlapping marks** (accumulation over time) | 2, honestly weak | The hardest one to iconify, consistent with it being the one axiom without a settled product yet either — flagged, not forced. |
| Change | Δ *(kept, already confirmed)* | 2 | Real, but borrowed — a triangle doesn't intrinsically mean "change" without calculus notation history. Kept because it's already load-bearing elsewhere in the project, not because it passes the strict test. |

## What this leaves open

- **Desire and Experience are the two honest weak points** — both are
  internal/temporal states that resist pure geometric iconicity. Worth
  deciding whether that's acceptable (not everything needs to pass the
  strictest possible test) or worth more design time specifically on
  these two.
- **Change (Δ) is kept on pragmatic grounds, not because it passes the
  stranded-human test** — flagged rather than quietly left ambiguous.
- **The operator set (4) is itself a proposal** — Escalation and
  Inversion in particular deserve real scrutiny since they're now
  doing structural work (resolving Attract/Radiate and Progression/
  Regression) rather than just decorating a single root.
