## Direct cross-references already visible against The 30

Noted here as observations, not resolutions — real structural clues
worth having in hand before actually reconciling the two lists:

- **Ω — "The Systems of Endings"** matches The 30's own confirmed
  glyph for **End (Ω)** exactly.
- **◬ — "The Systems of Subjective Realities"** shares its root
  character with The 30's confirmed **Progression (◬ш)** glyph. Since
  ◬ш was described there as shared with the now-merged Subjugation,
  this raises a real possibility: ◬ may be a *root* glyph from this
  list, with The 30's compound glyphs built by appending a modifier
  character (here, "ш") onto one of these 25 roots — not a
  coincidence, a real structural pattern to check against the rest of
  The 30 once more glyphs are available.
- **⟐ — "The Systems of Objective Truths"** lines up with
  `THE_30_RECLASSIFICATION.md`'s own independent note that the legacy
  ⟐ glyph was shared by "Objectivity/CoreOfChange" — same concept
  ("objective[ness]"), named from two separate sources.
- **Δ — "The Systems of Neutral, Change, and Undefined"** matches The
  30's confirmed **Change (Δ)** glyph, though this entry folds in
  Neutral and Undefined alongside Change — broader than The 30's
  Change entry alone, worth checking during reconciliation.
